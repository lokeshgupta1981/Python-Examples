# Expense Policy (Version 1)

## 3.1 Submitting expenses

Expenses must be submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the purchase, with a receipt for every item.

## 3.2 Meals during business travel

During business travel, meals are reimbursed up to 50 EUR per day in Germany and up to 3,000 INR per day in India.

## 3.3 Approval limits

Any single expense above 500 EUR or 40,000 INR needs approval from a director before the purchase.
