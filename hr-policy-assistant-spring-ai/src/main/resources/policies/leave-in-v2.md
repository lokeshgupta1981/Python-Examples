# Leave Policy India (Version 2)

## 1.1 Annual leave entitlement

Full-time employees in India get 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 1.5 days for each completed month of service.

## 1.2 Carrying leave forward

Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried forward into the next calendar year. Days above that limit lapse on 31 December.

## 1.3 Sick leave

Full-time employees in India get 8 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive working days.
