# Leave Policy Germany (Version 1)

## 1.1 Annual leave entitlement

Full-time employees in Germany get 28 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year, based on a five-day working week.

## 1.2 Carrying leave forward

Unused annual leave can be carried forward only until 31 March of the next calendar year. Leave not taken by then lapses.

## 1.3 Sick leave

Employees in Germany who are sick must inform their manager on the first day of absence. A doctor's certificate is required from the fourth calendar day of sickness.
