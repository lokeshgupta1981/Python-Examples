# Parental Leave Policy Germany (Version 1)

## 5.1 Parental leave

Employees in Germany can take parental leave of up to 3 years per child. The request must be made in writing at least 7 weeks before the leave starts.

## 5.2 Company top-up for partners

In addition to statutory parental leave, the company gives the second parent 10 working days of fully paid leave within 3 months of the birth.
