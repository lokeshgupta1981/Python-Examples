# Remote Work Policy (Version 1)

## 2.1 Working from home

Employees and contractors can work from home up to 3 days per week. The days are agreed with the manager in advance.

## 2.2 Working from another country

Working from a country other than the country of employment is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year. It needs written HR approval at least 15 days before the first day abroad.

## 2.3 Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a headset. Personal devices must not be used to store company documents.
