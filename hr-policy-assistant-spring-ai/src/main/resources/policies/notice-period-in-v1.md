# Notice Period Policy India (Version 1)

## 7.1 Notice during probation

During the 6-month probation period, the notice period for full-time employees in India is 30 days.

## 7.2 Notice after probation

After probation, the notice period for full-time employees in India is 60 days.

## 7.3 Shortening the notice period

The notice period can be shortened by buying out the remaining days, if the manager and HR agree in writing.
