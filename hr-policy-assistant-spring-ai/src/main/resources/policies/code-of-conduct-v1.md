# Code of Conduct (Version 1)

## 6.1 Respect at work

Everyone at the company, including contractors, treats colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment, bullying and discrimination are not tolerated.

## 6.2 Gifts

Gifts from customers or suppliers worth more than 50 EUR or 4,000 INR must be declared to the manager within 5 working days.

## 6.3 Reporting a concern

Concerns about behaviour at work are reported to HR at hr-help@example.com or through the anonymous ethics line. Nobody is penalised for raising a concern in good faith.
