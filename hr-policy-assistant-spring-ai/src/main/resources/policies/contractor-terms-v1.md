# Contractor Terms (Version 1)

## 8.1 Time off

Contractors do not get paid annual leave or paid sick leave. A contractor agrees days off with the project manager at least 10 working days in advance and invoices only the days actually worked.

## 8.2 Public holidays

Contractors are not paid for public holidays. Days when the office is closed are not invoiced.

## 8.3 Ending the contract

Either party can end a contractor agreement with 15 days of written notice.
