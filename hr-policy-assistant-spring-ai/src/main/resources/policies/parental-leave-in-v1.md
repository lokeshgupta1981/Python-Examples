# Parental Leave Policy India (Version 1)

## 5.1 Maternity leave

Full-time employees in India who give birth get 26 weeks of paid maternity leave.

## 5.2 Paternity and partner leave

Full-time employees in India get 10 working days of paid partner leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption of a child.
