# Notice Period Policy Germany (Version 1)

## 7.1 Notice during probation

During the 6-month probation period, either side can end employment in Germany with 2 weeks of notice.

## 7.2 Notice after probation

After probation, the notice period for employees in Germany is 3 months to the end of a calendar month.
